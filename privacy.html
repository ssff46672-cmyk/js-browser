Privacy Policy

JS Browser

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

JS Browser is a lightweight application designed for browsing web pages and exploring and testing web content with JavaScript.

2. Information We Collect

JS Browser does not intentionally collect, store, or sell personal information through its own application services.

The application may display websites and online content provided by third parties. Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms. JS Browser does not control the data practices of third-party websites.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Websites opened through JS Browser may use cookies, local storage, analytics technologies, or other similar technologies. These technologies are controlled by the websites you visit and their respective providers.

4. Third-Party Services

JS Browser may load content from third-party websites and services. Their collection and processing of information are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We aim to keep the application simple and do not intentionally operate a service that stores users' personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

JS Browser does not intentionally maintain a personal user database. Therefore, there is no personal account data stored by JS Browser that users need to request for deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

JS Browser is not specifically directed at children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the application's own services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the developer through the official JS Browser GitHub repository: JS Browser GitHub Repository

10. Application Identification

This Privacy Policy applies to the JS Browser application and its associated web version hosted at: JS Browser
